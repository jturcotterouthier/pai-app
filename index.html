PAI — Personal AI Infrastructure

A single-user personal assistant, run by and for Jérôme Turcotte-Routhier.

PAI is private tooling, not a product. It runs locally on hardware I own and automates my own admin: calendar, reminders, notes, reading, fitness data, and correspondence. There is no sign-up, no hosted service, and no other users.

What it connects to

  • Google Contacts — read and write, so the assistant can look up and maintain my own address book.
  • Gmail — read-only, so it can summarise and triage my own mail. It cannot send.

Both connections are to my personal Google Account and are authorised by me, for me. No one else has access.

Privacy

Google data accessed by PAI stays on my own machines. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party. See the privacy policy for the full statement.
